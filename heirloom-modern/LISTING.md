## About the maker

George Widdicomb trained as a cabinetmaker in England, emigrated in 1845, and opened a
cabinet shop in Grand Rapids in 1857 with his four sons. All four enlisted in the Civil War
and the shop failed in 1864 — then the brothers came home and started again. The firm was
incorporated as the Widdicomb Furniture Company in 1873, and in 1897 **John Widdicomb left to
found his own company**, the maker of this table.

Through the mid-century the Widdicomb firms drew the best designers in America — T.H.
Robsjohn-Gibbings, George Nakashima, and John Widdicomb's own chief designer J. Stuart
Clingman, whose sculpted walnut dining furniture defines the company's 1950s look.

The name retired in 2002 after 145 years. Nothing further was made — which is why what
survives is what there is.
