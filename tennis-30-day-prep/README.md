# Championship Countdown — 30-Day Tennis Prep App

A self-contained, single-file web app (`index.html`) that runs a research-backed 30-day
training block leading into a tennis club championship. Open it in any browser —
no build, no server, no dependencies. Progress and logged stats persist in the
browser's localStorage, with JSON export/import for backup.

Published artifact (private to the owner): https://claude.ai/code/artifact/fe408cab-7b67-46a5-84c5-ee33477e886f

## What's inside

- **Today** — day-by-day sessions (Day 1–30) with tap-to-complete checklists,
  a focus cue, a SwingVision instruction, and a video/reading assignment per day.
- **Plan** — the full 30-day calendar in four weekly phases:
  1. Baseline & Quick Fixes (film everything, pick ONE technique touch-up)
  2. Patterns & Pressure (serve +1, deep returns, stakes; technique freeze on Day 10)
  3. Match Simulation (style matches, two-set day, last hard fitness)
  4. Taper & Sharpen (~50% volume cut, match intensity, restring Day 24)
- **Stats** — a SwingVision-derived scorecard (1st serve %, double faults/set,
  winners:errors, depth %, serve-target %, weak-wing errors, 0–4 shot points won)
  with trend charts and club-level target bands.
- **Playbook** — the 16-second between-point routine, opponent archetype tactics
  (pusher / moonballer / big hitter / lefty), warm-up scouting checklist,
  match-day protocol (nutrition, warm-up, between-match recovery, bag checklist),
  SwingVision camera/workflow guide, and the coaching-channel curriculum.

## Research basis (summary)

The plan synthesizes:
- **Motor learning**: random/variable practice over blocked reps (better retention
  under pressure), external-focus cues over body cues, pressure training weekly
  from week 2, no stroke rebuilds inside 30 days (freeze by day 10).
- **Periodization**: compressed pre-competitive phase + taper — 70/30 on/off-court,
  rising intensity, falling volume; final week at 100% frequency / ~90% intensity /
  50% volume; full rest the day before round one.
- **Club-level match stats**: ~75% of club points end on errors; ~70% of points end
  within 4 shots; targets of 60–65% first serves, ≤2 double faults/set,
  winners:errors ≥ 0.8, 50%+ groundstrokes past the service line.
- **SwingVision workflows**: fixed camera position (behind baseline, centered,
  8–10 ft), Match/Serve Practice/Goal Mode usage, same-day error-reel review,
  weekly 45-min comparison ritual, day-25 personal scouting report.
- **Coaching sources**: Intuitive Tennis (serve), Feel Tennis (feel-based fixes,
  30-Day Serve Challenge), Essential Tennis (strategy/positioning), Top Tennis
  Training (drills, anti-pusher), 2Minute Tennis (quick fixes), Tennis Tribe
  (doubles), Tennis Troll (real club-level match film), plus *Winning Ugly*
  (Gilbert) and *The Inner Game of Tennis* (Gallwey).
